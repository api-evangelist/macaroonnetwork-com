# Moirai Labs

> Moirai Labs is a smart-contract analytics platform for Web3 projects with a first-class AI agent (Moirai Labs AI) exposing on-chain analytics over the A2A (Agent-to-Agent) protocol, MCP, and a public OpenAPI surface.

Moirai Labs turns raw blockchain data into product-grade analytics: contract metrics, method-level breakdowns, cohort analysis, and cross-chain views. The same data is available to humans (web dashboard) and to autonomous agents (A2A / MCP).
